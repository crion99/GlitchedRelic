# Relic artwork prompts

Generated with the built-in imagegen tool as transparent PNG game assets.
All twelve prompts requested centered, readable-at-64px, hand-painted 2D
dark-fantasy relic sprites on genuinely transparent backgrounds, with no text,
border, watermark, or copied Slay the Spire / Binding of Isaac item art.

- `blue_map.png`: folded cobalt-blue dungeon map with two red X marks and dotted corridors.
- `compass.png`: ornate brass compass with four bold directions and glowing blue center.
- `bogo_bombs.png`: paired black fantasy bombs tied by a red cord, with golden sparks.
- `pyromaniac.png`: charred heart in friendly orange flames with green healing glow.
- `ipecac.png`: squat green poison flask with skull-like cork and acidic droplet.
- `sacred_heart.png`: radiant crimson heart wrapped in gold thorn rays and white glow.
- `wafer.png`: thin pale circular protective wafer, cracked, with blue shield light.
- `holy_mantle.png`: white-and-gold sacred cloth mantle on a dark hook.
- `nine_volt.png`: compact brass-and-black nine-volt battery with blue spark.
- `halo.png`: floating gold halo casting warm light onto a small shield.
- `dataminer.png`: angular chipped crimson mining pickaxe with brass rivets,
  dark wood handle, and teal tip glimmer.
- `glitched_relic.png`: cracked red mining pickaxe embedded in a black-and-brass
  relic core with cyan and magenta glitch fragments.
